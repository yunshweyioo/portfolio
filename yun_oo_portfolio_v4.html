The mess

Notes came from every direction with no single version of the truth. Different leads held different information, artists redid work from conflicting notes — and a deliverable nearly reached the client as the wrong version.

What I built

One client-facing shot tracker, refreshed before every review, backed by per-lead ShotGrid views with status, priority, and OT-risk flags. Every note got one owner — nothing missed or done twice.

The result

When scope shifted mid-production, one update cascaded to every lead and the client saw the timeline impact instantly. A major reprioritization that would've caused chaos went through without missing a step.

Monarch: Legacy of Monsters · Apple TV+ · Series · ~1 year

Spotted a daily time-sink and rebuilt the show's naming system in a week

Production Coordinator · 1 supervisor, 1 lead, 20+ artists · tracked spend, POs & hour forecasts
The mess

Every client note had to be decoded before anyone could act on it — and shots nearly slipped through when the supervisor didn't realize they shared a sequence.

What I built

Without being asked, I mapped every code to its real name across the file directory and ShotGrid — priority sequences first — as a shared reference anyone could use. Done in a week without disrupting production.

The result

Reviews moved faster with no time lost translating codes. The supervisor started using the map directly in client calls, and it became the template for how they run shows.

Venom: The Last Dance · Sony Pictures · Feature Film · ~7 months

Turned an overnight-sync failure into a company-wide fix

Production Coordinator · 2 departments, LA + London teams
The mess

With an 8-hour gap between offices and no way to fix syncs ourselves, every failure meant hours lost waiting on IT — with a client review on the line.

What I built

Set up daily EOD summaries to keep both sides aligned. Then, with my supervisor's sign-off, I took IT a tight, low-effort brief — and won manual sync access for coordinators across both departments.

The result

Zero dropped handoffs — we resolved syncs ourselves instead of waiting on IT, and both LA and London supervisors praised the process.
